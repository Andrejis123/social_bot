# B2B Prospect List: Irish Social Media & Digital Marketing Market
**Research Date:** 28-07-2026

## Sourcing Methodology

This prospect list was compiled using targeted web searches (WebSearch) and selective page fetches (WebFetch) from agency directories (Clutch.co, SortList, Fetch.ie, DesignRush). Company contact routes are published role-based addresses (info@, hello@) or public contact forms found on company websites. No personal email addresses or direct phone numbers were collected. All Instagram handles were sourced from official company accounts only when readily discoverable via search. This research respects GDPR legitimate-interest principles - all contacts are business addresses and public company information suitable for B2B outreach.

## Key Findings Summary

**Track A Agencies:** 46 social media and digital marketing agencies identified across Ireland, concentrated in Dublin (20+) with strong secondary clusters in Cork (5), Galway (3), Limerick (3+), Clare (3), and secondary locations. Agencies range from boutique consultancies (1-3 person) to full-service multi-award-winning firms (Wolfgang, Evolution, Core Optimisation).

**Track B Brands:** 41 SMB consumer brands active on Instagram across food/drink, fashion/retail, beauty, jewelry, hospitality, fitness, and eco/sustainability sectors. Dublin dominates (15+) with meaningful presence in Galway (3), Cork (2), and rural locations. Instagram following ranges from 9.6K (niche jewelry) to 283K (Gym+Coffee fitness brand).

**Opportunity:** Brands and agencies alike show heavy reliance on social media without dedicated competitor monitoring tooling. Limited evidence of structured IG competitor tracking among SMB cohort - primarily reactive content strategy. High uptake of influencer collaboration but no systematic social-listening visible in public comms.
